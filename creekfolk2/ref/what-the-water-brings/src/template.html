What the Water Brings

The Philosopher King walks down to the creek to see what the water has brought: King’s Creek, the Dry Fork under the bluff, Teal Branch from its spring, the stock pond, the old road and the railroad. What he finds goes on the wall of the studio. Rest when the light goes long, sit out a storm, and look for five old shapes. In ten quiet places a poem is waiting, old ones about water, stars and the shapes of things.
